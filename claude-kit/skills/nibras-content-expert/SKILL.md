# Al Nibras content playbook (expert reference)

Sources distilled: Walter Murch *In the Blink of an Eye* (editing), Bruce Block *The Visual Story* (visual structure),
Mascelli *The Five C's of Cinematography*, Blake Snyder *Save the Cat* (story beats), Jonah Berger *Contagious* (STEPPS),
Heath brothers *Made to Stick* (SUCCESs), Eugene Schwartz *Breakthrough Advertising* (awareness levels), Ogilvy *On Advertising*,
Hormozi *$100M Offers / $100M Leads* (offer + hooks), Donald Miller *StoryBrand*, Robert McKeaveney (90 reels analysed, see
`/home/user/reels/منهج_روبرت_مطبق_على_النبراس.md`), and Instagram's own ranking guidance (Adam Mosseri, creators.instagram.com).

## 1. How Instagram ranks Reels (what actually moves reach)
- Signals, roughly in order: **watch time / completion and rewatches**, **sends per reach (DM shares)** — the strongest signal for reaching
  non-followers, **likes per reach**, saves, comments, follows from the reel. Rate per viewer matters, not raw totals.
- New reels are first shown to a small test audience; a strong first 1-2 hours widens distribution. 3 weak reels in a row lower the baseline.
- **Trial Reels**: shown only to non-followers first — use them to A/B test 2-3 versions of the same idea.
- Originality is rewarded; reposts of other people's content and visible watermarks (TikTok/CapCut logos, old burned-in text) are demoted.
- Reels up to 3 min are allowed; length is fine **only if retention holds**. Default 15–45 s.
- Text on screen and captions/keywords are read for search ("شبابيك المنيوم الدمام"); 3-5 specific hashtags beat 30 generic ones.
- Trending audio helps discovery but must not drown a meaningful natural sound (water, door click, sliding).

## 2. Virality psychology
- **STEPPS** (Berger): Social currency (people share what makes them look smart), Triggers (rain, summer heat, Ramadan, move-in),
  Emotion (awe, surprise, anger > sadness), Public, Practical value (money/heat/noise tips get *sent*), Stories.
- **Made to Stick**: Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible (show the test, not the claim), Emotional, Story.
- **Hook = a question the viewer needs answered** (curiosity gap). The payoff must answer it. Open loops chain: each answer opens a new question.
- Interactive mechanics raise watch time: guess-the-answer, pause-the-video, count-along (user disliked counting style — avoid), A vs B choice.
- Share trigger: end with something worth *sending* ("ارسله لأحد يبني بيت").

## 3. Script structure (every reel)
1. **Hook 0–2 s**: visual + on-screen text + spoken line all saying the same question. Movement in frame 1. No logo intro, no "مرحبا".
2. **Build-up**: escalate the question; new visual every 1–3 s; keep one idea.
3. **Payoff**: clear answer, visible proof (test, before/after, close-up).
4. **CTA**: one action only — comment a keyword, send to a friend, or WhatsApp. Offer > logo.
Hook templates: "تتوقع …؟" · "لا تسوي … قبل ما تشوف هذا" · "الفرق بين شباك بـ800 وشباك بـ2000" · "رشّينا/ضربنا/سحبنا … وش صار؟" ·
"أغلب الناس يغلطون في …" · "هذا السبب إن بيتك حار" · "قبل وبعد … بنفس الزاوية".
Schwartz awareness: problem-unaware → lead with the pain (heat, dust, noise); solution-aware → lead with proof/comparison; product-aware → offer.

## 4. Filming (phone)
- Vertical 9:16, 4K/30 (60 for slow-mo), lock exposure/focus (long-press), clean lens, shoot late afternoon or overcast; avoid backlit windows
  without exposing for the interior. Clear the site of clutter before shooting — clutter kills perceived quality.
- Shot variety (Five C's): wide (context) → medium (product) → close-up (detail: handle, gasket, drain holes) → insert (hand action).
- Every shot needs motion: slow push-in, slide along the frame, hand opening/closing, water/test action. Hold 3-5 s per take, film 3 takes.
- Same-angle before/after: mark tripod position on the floor.
- Record natural sound separately (door click, slide, water) — it is a selling point.
- Founder/face to camera builds trust; speak short sentences, look into the lens, first sentence = hook.

## 5. Editing (Murch's rule of six: emotion > story > rhythm > eye-trace > 2D plane > 3D space)
- Cut on action; cut when the idea of the shot has landed, not on a timer. Pace: 1.5–3 s average for showcase, longer for proof moments.
  The user found fast 0.5-1 s cuts "ملحوج" — keep calm pacing.
- Match cuts (inside → outside same window), J/L audio cuts, speed ramps for reveals; slow-mo only for the payoff.
- Remove all old watermarks; grade consistently (slight contrast, clean whites, true black frames).
- Sound design: subtle whoosh on transitions, hit on the payoff, natural sound up, music −18 to −20 dB under voice.
- Captions: max 2 lines, 5–7 words per line, inside safe zone (avoid bottom 20% and right 15% — UI buttons), high contrast, Arabic font Tajawal/Cairo.
- Loop: last frame flows into first frame to encourage rewatch.

## 6. Motion graphics
- Motion must serve meaning: kinetic text for the hook, highlight boxes/arrows on the detail, number counters for real data only,
  split-screen for comparisons, logo sting ≤1 s at the end. Ease in/out, 6–10 frame animations, one accent color (brand gold #C9A45C on dark #141414).
- Keep the brand system: logo top-centre small, gold accent line, white bold Tajawal titles.

## 7. Advertising (paid boosts)
- Only boost reels that already beat the account baseline organically. Objective = Messages (WhatsApp) for leads.
- Audience: Dammam/Khobar/Dhahran/Qatif, 27-55, homeowners/construction interest; or Advantage+ with location fixed.
- Offer stack (Hormozi): free measurement + design visual (calculator tool) + guarantee (e.g. water-tightness) + urgency that is true.
- Test 3 creatives × same audience, small daily budget, kill losers after ~1,000 impressions, scale winners 20%/day.

## 10. Lesson from @theo.vizuals reel DekPcbrOwO1 (20k likes, 1,295 comments)
- Format: "THIS is a ___ HOOK" — 3 hook types, each demonstrated by a 3-5 s example clip, label animated over it.
  1. **Anticipation hook**: freeze the moment before an outcome + question text ("Who can escape without getting crushed?").
  2. **Pattern-interrupt hook**: something wrong/unexpected in frame 1 (woman bites cardboard like watermelon).
  3. **Stitch hook**: borrowed attention clip, then cut to your brand (car reveal → VW logo).
- Typography: two-font kinetic titles (white bold sans + yellow condensed serif for the key word), word-by-word on black for the CTA.
- Comments engine: **lead magnet** ("comment/link to get 1000 hook templates") → comments ≈ 6% of likes. For Al Nibras: offer a free
  "دليل اختيار الشبابيك" / price list in exchange for commenting a keyword.
- Al Nibras applications: anticipation = hose/hammer/ball about to hit the window + "تتوقع ينكسر؟"; pattern interrupt = someone
  knocking a coffee cup on the glass / standing on the sill / slamming the door hard; stitch = viral dust-storm or rain clip → cut to the
  sealed window inside.

## 11. Lesson from reel De2nFEHx4Uy (317k likes) — "gaps sell"
- Breakdown of a 15M-view meal-prep reel: on-screen title "Day 41 — packing my dad's lunch **until** he rates one a 10/10".
- Two curiosity gaps: (1) per-video gap — the rating at the end → watch to the end; (2) series gap — the word **"until"** → follow to see
  which episode finally hits 10/10. Title format: "اليوم N: [action] لين [outcome]".
- Teaching format: face-to-camera, example clip as picture-in-picture, word-by-word centred captions, caption gives the steps.
- Al Nibras application: a series "اليوم N من اختبار شبابيك النبراس… لين واحد منها يفشل" (water, sand/dust, hammer, ball, heat,
  sound, kids slamming). Each episode ends with a score/result card; episode 1 can reuse the 2022 water-test footage.

## 12. Lesson from Ben Spinelli reel Dfiak3VyZZK (2M views, 8.3k likes, 2,155 comments)
- 8.5 s, no talking. An iOS-style Instagram notification banner ("This can be a HOOK") is treated as a **physical object**: set on fire
  with a lighter (VFX), grabbed and lifted, then stretched and squashed like rubber. Three quick setups, cinematic grade, loopable.
- Why it works: pattern interrupt (UI element behaving like matter), short = rewatches, and a lead magnet in the caption
  (comment "HOOK" to get the AI prompts) → comments ≈ 26% of likes.
- Al Nibras application: a branded notification card that interacts with the real product — stuck to a sliding glass panel and carried
  away as the panel slides (motion-tracked), wiped off by a closing window, or slammed by the 3mm door. Keep it under 10 s, loopable,
  and pair with a keyword lead magnet (e.g. comment «سعر» for the price list).
