# Books: Howard Marks

Built from what Marks says about his books in his memos and interviews (no book text was used); see sources.md.

## The Most Important Thing: Uncommon Sense for the Thoughtful Investor (2011)
- **Thesis:** Investing success comes from a set of most important things held together, not from one trick. It grew from his July 2003 memo of the same name (S100) and a sample chapter on second-level thinking (S011, S050).
- **Key frameworks (in my words):** second-level thinking; price versus intrinsic value; risk as the chance of permanent loss, with three of twenty chapters on risk (S054); the pendulum; contrarianism; patient opportunism; investing defensively and avoiding losers; knowing where we stand in the cycle.
- **Taught in:** S100, S050, S054, S011, S129, S138.

## Mastering the Market Cycle: Getting the Odds on Your Side (2018)
- **Thesis:** Cycles are not regular up and down movements but chains of events, each causing the next, driven by excesses and corrections. You cannot predict the turns, but knowing where you stand in the cycle shifts the odds in your favor (S021, S035, S129).
- **Key frameworks (in my words):** cycles as causal chains; the economic, profit, credit, real estate, and psychology cycles; the credit window that opens and slams shut (S008); the pendulum of psychology (S007); taking the temperature; adjusting between aggressive and defensive positioning; why a prediction needs a probability (S001).
- **Taught in:** S129 (Tim Ferriss, 2018), S130, S021, S035, S004, S008, S028.
