# Books: Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Built from his public talks, interviews, free essays, and the free arXiv edition of the Technical Incerto. No book text is reproduced here; summaries are in our words.

## Dynamic Hedging (1997)
- **Thesis:** A practitioner's manual on managing option books. The core lesson he keeps citing: traders must avoid the absorbing barrier, because once bust you cannot continue.
- **Key ideas:** Path dependence, convexity, the gap between models and pit trading.
- **Taught in:** S020 (section 3.11), S055.

## Fooled by Randomness (2001)
- **Thesis:** We mistake luck for skill and noise for signal, especially in markets. Survivorship bias makes winners look wise.
- **Key ideas:** Survivorship, the turkey, the difference between being right and making money, Fat Tony's sucker test.
- **Taught in:** S060, S051, S053, S052.

## The Black Swan (2007, 2nd ed. 2010)
- **Thesis:** Rare, high-impact events dominate history and markets; we cannot predict them, so build things that survive them. Mediocristan versus Extremistan.
- **Key ideas:** Fat tails, the turkey problem, the narrative fallacy, the ludic fallacy, the expert problem, the fourth quadrant (2nd edition essay on robustness).
- **Taught in:** S043, S045, S048, S012, S056, S053, S060.

## The Bed of Procrustes (2010)
- **Thesis:** A book of aphorisms on how we cut reality to fit our models.
- **Taught in:** lightly, S084, S067 (aphorism style).

## Antifragile (2012)
- **Thesis:** Some things gain from disorder. Remove fragility first, then add optionality. The barbell, via negativa, Lindy, and the fragility heuristic as practical tools.
- **Key ideas:** Fragile, robust, antifragile; concave versus convex harm; optionality; skin in the game appears at the end as the people who make others fragile.
- **Taught in:** S078, S075, S072, S081, S047, S017, S010, S023.

## Skin in the Game (2018)
- **Thesis:** Those who take decisions must bear the downside. Skin in the game is a filter that keeps systems honest and is also how you read belief and expertise. The last chapter, "The Logic of Risk Taking," on ergodicity and ruin, he calls the one to read if you read nothing else.
- **Key ideas:** The Bob Rubin trade, the silver rule, the minority rule, Lindy, ergodicity and the uncle point, belief as risk taking.
- **Taught in:** S052, S079, S054, S055, S050, S068, S016, S009, S069, S022.

## Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails (Technical Incerto, vol. 1, 2020, free on arXiv)
- **Thesis:** Standard statistics break under fat tails: the law of large numbers works too slowly, sample means and correlations mislead, and risk must be read in payoff space.
- **Key ideas:** Preasymptotics, kappa metric, shadow mean, the hidden tail, catastrophe principle, ruin and path dependence, MPT fallacies, correlation as a poor metric.
- **Taught in:** S020 (the book itself, free edition), S051, S031, S035, S038.
