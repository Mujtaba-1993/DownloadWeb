# Books: Edward O. Thorp

Built from his papers, columns and interviews that discuss the books (S002, S004, S006, S022, S027, S028, S029). No book text was used.

## Beat the Dealer (1962, revised 1966)
- **Thesis:** Blackjack can be beaten because the deck has memory. Track the cards that have gone, and bet more when the remaining deck favors you.
- **Key ideas:** Card counting; a conditional, measurable edge; the first public use of Kelly betting for bankroll management. (S004, S033, S010)

## Beat the Market (1967, with Sheen Kassouf)
- **Thesis:** Warrants and convertibles can be valued with math and hedged against the common stock, giving market-neutral returns.
- **Key ideas:** Hedge ratios; buy underpriced and short overpriced related securities; the "avalanche" effect of rebalancing with margin. (S004, S006, S029)

## The Mathematics of Gambling (1984)
- **Thesis:** Collected analyses of casino games and money management, including Kelly betting. Its columns ran in Gambling Times (S033, S034).

## The Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion (2011, with MacLean and Ziemba)
- **Thesis:** The definitive collection on Kelly betting: its good properties (fastest long-run growth, no ruin) and bad ones (very large, volatile bets in the short run). (S001, S031, S022)

## A Man for All Markets (2017)
- **Thesis:** Memoir from blackjack to hedge funds. Lessons: think for yourself, test ideas in the real world, size bets with Kelly, most people should index. (S027, S028, S029, S006)

## Elementary Probability (1966)
- A textbook. Not used in this brain.

Major papers: A Favorable Strategy for Twenty-One (1961, S032), Portfolio Choice and the Kelly Criterion (1971, S005), The Kelly Criterion and the Stock Market (1992, S013), The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market (2006, S004), A Perspective on Quantitative Finance (2003, S002), the Statistical Arbitrage series in Wilmott (2004 to 2005, S016 to S020).
